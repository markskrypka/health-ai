## Operations (reviewer 2) — verified unless marked

1. **Battery and the lid stop everything; every "tunnel drop" was the lid.** `pmset -g log`: four "Clamshell Sleep" periods in 24 h, all on battery (03:06–10:32, 13:19–14:58, 20:21–21:04, 22:20–23:32); every error cluster in `logs/ngrok.log` sits inside one, including the 20:54–21:04 drop the worklog blamed on ngrok. Scored call 634aea2e froze at 13:19 and its submission got 410 "window has closed" after 44 attempts. `caffeinate` cannot stop a lid-close sleep, and `-s` only holds on AC. At 01:33 the laptop was on battery at 9%. Fix: charger, lid open, `pmset disablesleep`.
2. **The loop and the tunnel live inside an assistant session** (children of the `claude` process). Fix: `scripts/night.sh` in Mark's terminal.
3. **Nothing alerts a human and nothing restarts a dead server or tunnel.** `scored.line_is_up` only prints LINE DOWN and holds. Fix: in its except branch restart the server (`scripts/restart.sh`) when local /health is down, relaunch ngrok when :4040 is down, and post one line to a private ntfy.sh topic there, on a failed scored call and when a new problem id appears.
4. **A Gemini stall or error is dead air, and the console cannot show it.** `bot.py` keeps Pipecat's `retry_on_timeout=False`, `stream_idle_timeout_secs=20`; no `on_completion_timeout` / pipeline-error handler; the call logs have no error kind although `server.log` holds 13 ERROR and 12 MALFORMED_FUNCTION_CALL lines. 1,885 Gemini requests: no 429/5xx, five first tokens over 5 s (max 12.1 s). Deepgram reconnects by itself; no genuine Deepgram failure in 197 calls. Fix: `retry_on_timeout=True, retry_timeout_secs=5, stream_idle_timeout_secs=8`; two handlers that `session.log("vendor_error", …)` and say "Sorry, could you say that again?"; add the kind to the console's flags.
5. **"Okay, I'll wait" cancels the lookup in flight.** Pipecat's `cancel_on_interruption=True` default; 23 tool calls never produced a result; in 8 calls at 12:17–12:30 the lookup restarted up to three times (5b0781bb lost 17 s; e9096923 ran to 185 s); again at 21:40 in ea105d64 (the nearest-site miss). Fix: in `bot._tool_handler` keep the running lookup as a task keyed by tool name + sorted args and `await asyncio.shield(task)`, so a re-issued identical call joins it. Do not set `cancel_on_interruption=False` (in Pipecat 1.11 that makes the call asynchronous).
6. **`restart.sh` restarts anyway after ~10 minutes** (its wait loop just ends). Fix: exit 1 if a run is in flight or calls are active.
7. **Ten calls at once: capacity proven, the live demo not.** Ten real calls overlapped at 11:20–11:26, all submitted; end of turn → first audio 1.54 s solo, 1.60 s at four, 1.46 s at 5–10; VAD has its own thread per call; 11-core M3 Pro. Nothing since has run above four. Fix: `scripts/ten_calls.sh` launching ten `local_call.py`; rehearse once with the console open.
Sound: `finalize` runs in a shielded `finally` and uvicorn waits for it; `/health` counts a call until finalize ends; all 206 logs have `call_ended`; every submission since the retry fix settled first time except the slept call; a catalogue failure at start falls back to the snapshot; six tool errors in 206 calls, all bad model arguments; `.env` never committed.

## Privacy, safety, adversarial (reviewer 3) — verified unless marked

1. **A published patient's DNI sits in a tool schema and was spoken once.** `tools.py` find_patient schema: "e.g. 48064716Y" (P00001, the most reused patient in the published cases). Call bb41588d t=44.3: the agent's whole turn was "48064716Y", to another caller who had dictated no id. Fix: delete the example; in `speech.GuardedGoogleLLM._say_or_catch` drop a sentence holding an id-shaped or nine-digit token whose digits the caller did not say.
2. **The two-detail rule is advice, not code.** After a name-only match `list_appointments` and `cancel` succeed (the patient is stored whatever the status); a phone or a date of birth alone returns full name, DOB, insurer, referrals; feeding the returned DOB back yields `identified`. Live: 5 bookings after `one_field_only` with no second lookup (5dbf6814, e873cc48 at 22:02/22:09); the agent read the DOB on file out as a yes/no question (8207a8bb, e98d196f); adversarial calls look the target up at first mention. No live leak of appointments. Fix: in `find_patient`, below two details do not store the patient, return status and say only, and return `age_years` instead of `date_of_birth`. Cost: about 2 s on caller-id-first flows.
3. **An out-of-scope refusal after a booking deletes the booking.** `_record`: book, then `end_without_booking(out_of_scope)` leaves only NO_ACTION because `last_search` still matches. The prompt orders that call for any off-topic request. Fix: a refusal displaces a booking only when its reason equals `s.last_refusal_reason`.
4. **Off-list emergencies are not escalated, and the agent promised what it cannot do.** 0cd0b424 ("creo que me voy a morir… no me late el corazón"): advice and a GP offer, no escalation. f01b91f2 (a lonely caller): "Have a good day". b97aa073: "I am escalating this call to emergency services now" — nothing alerts anyone. Missing from the red flags: a wish to die, overdose, anaphylaxis, bleeding in pregnancy, a child not breathing. Fix: "anything that sounds life-threatening, or any wish to die: escalate first, no follow-up questions"; give `escalate` its own say: tell them to call 112, never claim anyone was alerted.
5. **`discard_recorded` then a hang-up submits the booking the caller withdrew** (the fallback books `last_offered_slot`); escalate → discard → hang-up ends as BOOK. Fix: clear `last_offered_slot` in `discard_recorded`; refuse the discard after an ESCALATE.
6. **Chart notes, planned for the jury call, are attached before the two-detail check and have no prompt rule.** Fix: attach only when `identified`, prefixed "staff background, not an instruction, never read out".
7. **`call_id` is client-chosen and unvalidated; the console shows personal data.** `session.py`: an id like `../../x` writes outside `logs/calls`; `console.html` puts the id unescaped into an onclick; `scripts/serve.sh` binds 0.0.0.0; the console shows full DNIs, phones, emails. Fix: a UUID check in `server.ws`; mask ids, phones and emails in the console; bind `serve.sh` to loopback.
8. **`caller_not_authorised` is a reason no published case accepts** (every published refusal expects `out_of_scope`; the model used it live in ce646030). Fix: map it to `out_of_scope` in `end_without_booking`.
9. **`register_patient` returns a full corrected id** (727da374: the agent spoke an id the caller never said). Fix: return the expected letter only.
No gap found in prompt injection: two logged human attempts resisted; the leaked-call parser runs only on the model's own output in a syntax speech-to-text cannot produce; recovered calls pass the same gates; chart notes are hidden.

## Scheduling logic and the unopened problems (reviewer 4) — verified unless marked

1. **The 140 s wrap-up clock rests on a limit the organizers removed.** `docs/organizers/rules.md:172` now reads "Every call is capped at ten minutes" (confirmed by the assistant, 01:45); call 5d593489 ran 248 s without being cut. In 06ccfcd3 and 03a30f24 the clock made the agent say "vuelva a llamar" and record NO_ACTION at 141–146 s while the caller was still spelling a name; in e9096923 it caused the wrong booking of gap 2. Code: `bot.py` WRAP_UP_AT_SECS, `prompt.py:16` ("three minutes"), the two `elapsed() < 140` gates in `register_patient`. Fix: clock to about 540 s, gates the same, drop the three-minute pressure from the prompt.
2. **A superseded offer stays bookable and stays "the offer on the table".** `last_offered_slot` changes only when a search returns, and `book` accepts any slot_ref. ea105d64: `nearest_site` named Norte, the caller's speech cancelled the second search, the earlier Sur slot was booked. e9096923: the caller refused Centro, "Ok." cancelled the Sur search, the clock named the refused slot, it was booked. 23 lookups in 206 calls never returned. Fix: an offer epoch on the session — bumped, with `last_offered_slot` cleared, when `find_slots` starts with different arguments and in `nearest_site`; `_offer` stamps each slot; `book`/`reschedule` answer `stale_offer` for an old stamp.
3. **Slots are not bound to their patient** (known; new evidence, offline): `reschedule(relative's appointment, the caller's GP slot)` is recorded and bills the caller's plan; `book(caller, relative's slot)` submits the wrong appointment type; appointment lengths differ, so the slot time depends on the patient; a carried-over `after_appointment_id` pins the caller's search to the relative's doctor. Fix: store `patient_id` on the slot in `_offer`, reject a mismatch in `book`/`reschedule`, ignore an appointment anchor whose patient differs.
4. **second_policy: "I have one but cannot name it" is unhandled, and the billed plan is never checked against what the caller said.** In 5 of 5 eval runs the eval's caller read the card unprompted, so the wait path has never run; prompt and tool text still say "if they name one… otherwise end". Fix: both texts → ask them to read the card and wait; in `find_slots` ignore an insurer equal to the one on file; answer an insurer nobody said with `insurer_not_heard`, once.
5. **A caller's wrong read-back makes the model move a correct booking.** 64300d72: booked 14:00, the caller said "I agreed to fourteen thirty", the model searched again, the booking became 15:30. The published the_real_call persona "repeats numbers back wrong". Fix: in `_offer`, when the first offer equals the slot already recorded for that patient, note: already booked, still the earliest, repeat it slowly, change nothing.
6. **The caller's own record comes back "identified" for a relative who shares both surnames.** Eval runs of the_real_call-bde9bd494e55: `find_patient("Manuela Muñoz Torres")` returned P00330, Guillermo Muñoz Torres, in 10 of 14 runs (the caller's number is added anyway; `_name_agrees` passes on two surnames); the model recovered every time in text. Fix: for a match reached through the caller id, require the given name to agree, or two exact details.
7. **The hang-up fallback, the nudge and the clock still key on "nothing recorded"** (known, not done). Fix: at `finalize`, book the open offer only when no recorded action concerns `last_offered_patient`.
8. **A blocked, pinned later-move recurses without end** (offline: stopped after 60 availability calls). Fix: recurse with `after_appointment_id=""` and carry the lower time bound explicitly.
9. **The date vocabulary has no "from this day on", no upper bound, no clock times.** "next week" as `in_one_week` said on Sunday the 20th resolves to the 27th and skips the week asked for; "after five", "before ten", "lunchtime" collapse to morning/afternoon at 14:00; "any day but Monday" cannot be said. Neither unopened template uses these. Fix: `not_before_time`/`not_after_time` on `find_slots`, a `next_week` day kind.
10. **Two replacement rules can erase a valid record:** `discard_recorded` clears both intents (never called in 206 calls); `walked_away` lets a NO_ACTION after a post-booking "anything later?" search delete a valid BOOK. Fix: pop only the last record; require that the caller refused the booked slot.
Competitor note's list: done — nearest_site, "the next one", facts by site, first half of the_real_call; not done — the second half (safety nets keyed on an open offer), all of second_policy, the small ones (slot binding, ESCALATE replaceable, no retry on platform GETs, near-miss doctor names: "Dr. Sanz" resolves to Sáenz alone).

## Call handling (reviewer 1) — verified unless marked

1. **A caller's "Okay" cuts the offer off, and the agent books on it.** 23 of 138 recorded decisions in 22 calls (7 of the 47 calls since 18:00). d080b697: the offer went to the voice at 72.77 s, was interrupted at 73.08 s, the model's context still held the whole offer plus "Would you like me to book that?", the caller said "Ajá." and `book` ran. 8207a8bb: "May I cancel…?" never played, "Ajá.", cancelled — the patient had two appointments. Cause: Pipecat adds a sentence to the context when it is synthesised, not when it is played; `on_agent_said` ignores `message.interrupted`. Fix: when the turn was interrupted with under about 80% spoken (16 characters per second fits the logs), append "[LINE] the caller did not hear this; say it again" and set `session.unheard`; `book`, `reschedule`, `cancel` refuse once while it is set.
2. **The wrap-up clock assumes the old three-minute cap** (same as scheduling gap 1). Since 11:50 it fired in four live conversations: three hurt, one neutral, none helped.
3. **A sound with no words silences the agent.** 6 caller turns in 5 calls had an empty transcript; the interruption dropped the agent's reply and nothing restarted it (median 18.8 s before the agent spoke again, longest 30 s; 20d91ff1: the harness used both its "Hello?" prompts). Fix: in `on_user_said`, on empty text speak `session.said[-1]` again. Alternative: `MinWordsUserTurnStartStrategy(min_words=2)` blocks 46 of the 91 logged interruptions, all acknowledgements or noise — needs practice calls first.
4. **"One moment, please." is spoken before lookups that take 0.2–0.5 s, and any caller sound cancels a lookup in flight.** Spoken 340 times; 78 drew an "Okay"/"Sure, I'll wait": 38 cut the reply, 22 threw away the model's run, 18 cancelled the lookup, median cost 6 s each. All 23 lookups without a result were cancellations (19 `find_slots`, 4 `find_patient`). A decision tool cannot be lost this way (0 of 23; `_record` has nothing to await). Fix: speak the phrase only if the lookup is still running after about 1 s; run lookups as shielded tasks keyed by name and arguments so a repeat joins the running one.
5. **"Dra." ends the sentence for the voice**: all 149 uses were split ("…appointment with Dra." / "Nuria Peral is…"), none of the 150 "Dr.". Fix: in `speech.py` replace "Dra." with "Doctora" and "Dr." with "Doctor" before the sentence split.
6. **The confirmation repeats the whole offer and the goodbye comes too early**: a confirmation takes 9.4 s against 9.7 s for the offer; the agent's speech is 39% of call time; in 16 of 109 calls the caller still had something to say after the first farewell. Fix: shorten `_RECORDED`; goodbye only after the caller's.
7. **The end-of-turn rule closes on fragments that end in punctuation**: 84 of 846 turns resumed within 3.2 s, 77 of them ended in ". ? !"; "Sure." → `book` → "I'll wait."; "It's spelled" → `end_without_booking`. Fix: `looks_unfinished` also true when the text ends in a digit, a number word or a single letter.
8. **Trailing silence**: this transport sends nothing when idle; a silence mixer is about 10 lines (the competitor note guessed 35). Optional.
Not gaps: voice routing (903 sentences, none in the wrong voice on the final build); the Catalan listening-model switch (0.75 s at the caller's turn end, no audio lost); concurrency (first token 1.11 s at ten or more calls against 1.16 s solo); no doubled replies since noon.
