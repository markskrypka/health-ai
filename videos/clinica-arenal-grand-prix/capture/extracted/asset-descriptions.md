# Asset inventory

- `apps/web/app/call/page.tsx` — the real patient-facing browser phone: optional identity form, waveform, transcript, appointment calendar, and per-call pipeline choice.
- `apps/web/app/desk/page.tsx` — the real live front-desk console: calls, transcript, action cards, patient panel, insights, pipeline comparison, and ten-call replay.
- `apps/web/public/patients/` — synthetic patient portraits used by the interface.
- `docs/demo.md` — the jury runbook and source for verified product claims.
- Screen recordings and still captures will be generated from the running upstream application and staged under `assets/ui/`.

No external website assets were captured. The product is the local application in this repository, and its real rendered screens are the visual source of truth.
